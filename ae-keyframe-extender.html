AE Keyframe Extender

Retime a linear two-keyframe move to new head/tail timecodes at the same constant speed.

Only linear (constant-velocity) motion is supported. For every property, only the first and last keyframe in the pasted data are used as the linear pair — anything in between is ignored.

Format: HH:MM:SS:FF (AE-style short forms like 0:00:00:00 are also accepted).
